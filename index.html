YTMP4 bookmarklet: YouTube to MP4 in one click

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar. On any YouTube video, click it and YT to MP4 opens with the video ready, so you only pick a quality and save the MP4.

YT to MP4

Bookmarks bar hidden? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on Mac).

Add it by hand

Create a new bookmark, name it YT to MP4 and paste this as the URL:

javascript:(()=>{const u=location.href;const y=/^https?:\/\/((www|m|music)\.)?(youtube\.com|youtu\.be)\//.test(u);window.open('https://yt-to-mp4.net/'+(y?'?url='+encodeURIComponent(u):''),'_blank','noopener')})()

On a phone

Bookmarklets are fiddly on mobile, so use the address bar trick instead. On a YouTube video, change youtube.com to yt-to-mp4.net and open it:

https://yt-to-mp4.net/watch?v=VIDEO_ID

Shorts work the same way: yt-to-mp4.net/shorts/VIDEO_ID.

What it does

It reads the page address, checks it is a YouTube link and opens the converter in a new tab with that link. Nothing else is sent or stored, and no permissions are needed. Source code: github.com/dipak8080/ytmp4-bookmarklet.

Only download videos you own or have permission to save. YT to MP4 is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
